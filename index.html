I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Paris Smaragdis.

I work on making audio models more efficient and controllable through structured representations.

My current research focuses on audio autoencoders: how to structure their representations, adapt their temporal resolution to the signal, and process audio directly in latent space, including bandwidth extension and stereo upmixing. I also study equivariant representations, where audio transformations correspond to simple, predictable latent operations. Earlier, I worked on source separation and generative modeling, including investigating training-data replication in audio diffusion models.
